Some Loombre libraries can be marked as restricted — set up by whoever runs
your Loombre for content that shouldn't be visible to everyone in the
household by default, or that needs an extra step before it's shown.
Nothing about this is meant to be uncomfortable to use; here's exactly how
it works.

## What "restricted" means

A restricted item stays completely invisible to you — not in search, not
on the home screen, nowhere — until every one of the following is true:

1. **Restricted content is turned on for your Loombre at all.** It's off
   by default. Ask whoever set up your Loombre if you're not sure.
2. **You're old enough.** Your account needs a birth date on file showing
   you're an adult.
3. **You've chosen to opt in, yourself.** Nobody can do this for you,
   including whoever administers your Loombre — you turn it on and set
   your own PIN, separate from your regular password. No one else can see
   or set that PIN.
4. **You've been given access to that specific library.** Being an adult
   who has opted in isn't enough by itself — whoever administers your
   Loombre also has to grant you access to each restricted collection.
5. **You've entered your PIN since you last signed in.** Even with all of
   the above, restricted content stays locked until you enter your PIN,
   and it locks again automatically after a while.

## Unlocking

The first time you try to open restricted content since you last signed
in, you'll be asked for your PIN. Once you enter it correctly, restricted
content stays available for a while — after that, it locks again
automatically and you'll be asked for your PIN the next time. You can also
lock it again yourself at any time if you want to put it away sooner.

## Your privacy

Restricted content is designed to be genuinely private, not just hidden
behind a click:

- It never appears in search results, browsing, or "recently added" for
  anyone who hasn't passed every step above — including other people on
  the same Loombre.
- Nothing about a restricted item — its title, its image, even that it
  exists — is sent to a device that hasn't unlocked it.

## Turning this on

If you'd like restricted content available to you and it isn't yet, ask
whoever administers your Loombre — they'll need to grant you access to the
specific library, and then you can opt in and set your own PIN from your
account settings.
