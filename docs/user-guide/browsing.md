When you open Loombre, you'll see your home screen: rows of movies, shows,
and music, organized to help you find something quickly.

[SCREENSHOT: Loombre home screen showing recently-added and continue-watching rows]

## Home screen

Near the top, you'll usually find a row of things you've started but
haven't finished — pick any of them up right where you left off. Below
that, rows show what's new in your library.

What you see here is personal to you. If the person who runs your Loombre
has set up restricted content or separate collections for different people
in the household, your home screen only shows what you're allowed to see.

## Browsing by type

Along the top or side of the screen, you'll find pills for each of your
libraries — separate ones for **Movies**, **TV Shows**, **Music**, or any
other collections your library is split into. Pick one to switch to it,
and use the sort control to order what you see: Recently Added, Title
A–Z, or Highest Rated.

[SCREENSHOT: Browse view for a movies library, showing poster grid and sort controls]

## Searching

Type into the search box and results appear as you type — no need to
press enter or wait. Search looks across movies, shows, music, and the
people in them (actors, directors, artists), so searching for an actor's
name can be just as useful as searching for a title.

[SCREENSHOT: Search-as-you-type showing live results]

## Looking at something before you watch it

Select anything to see its details page: a description, cast and crew (for
movies and shows) or track listing (for music), release year, and — for
shows — a list of seasons and episodes.
